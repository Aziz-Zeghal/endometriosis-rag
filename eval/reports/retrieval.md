## Limits

- Small sample (tens of questions per profile): one question moves Hit@5 by several points, so gaps under about 10 points are within noise.
- The lay / medical gap on matched pairs rests on few pairs (9 patient, 3 GP): one question is 11 points on the patient pairs.
- Questions are searched as typed. In the app, `rewrite` only changes follow-ups, so follow-up items are left out here.
- A hit means one reference document is retrieved, not that the passage answering the question is; RAGAS context precision covers that (eval/reports/ragas.md).
