## Limits

- RAGAS prompts are in English and the answers in French: read the scores as relative between configurations, not as absolute quality.
- Small sample (tens of questions per profile) and one run: the generator runs at temperature 0.2, so scores move between runs.
- Faithfulness counts every statement, including the fixed advice the prompt requires (« parlez-en à un médecin ») when no passage states it, which lowers the score of otherwise grounded answers.
- Answer relevancy is a cosine between the question and questions the judge generates from the answer; Nemotron cosines between paraphrases sit well below 1, so the scale is compressed. Answers that decline part of a request (diagnosis, dose) are scored as less relevant by design.
- Follow-up items are scored on the last message alone (the judge does not see the history).
